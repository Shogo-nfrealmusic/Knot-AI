# Interview prompt: build your CLAUDE.md

Copy CLAUDE.md from this starter into your notes folder, run Claude Code from that folder, and paste:

```text
Help me write the CLAUDE.md for my second brain.
Use the structure in the CLAUDE.md template that's already in this folder.
Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer before the next one.
Cover: who I am and what I do; my one goal for this year and how I'll measure it;
my current projects and their priority order; how I make decisions; the biases I know I have;
how you should talk to me; when you should disagree with me; and what you must never do without asking.
When we're done, fill in the template.
Keep it under 200 lines. Mark anything I didn't answer as TBD instead of guessing.
```

After the interview, start a fresh session and ask: "What's my priority this month, and what should you push back on?" If the answer is off, fix the file, not the chat.
